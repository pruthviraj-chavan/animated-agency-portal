## 1. Strategic Architecture: Two Independent Pillar Clusters Under One Trusted Brand

DieVekter Tech serves two high-value audiences that should never cannibalize or dilute each other in search:

1. **Vertical A — Technology & Software Solutions**  
   *Audience:* Startups, retail businesses, educational institutions, clinics, real estate teams and SMEs seeking reliable engineering.  
   *Pillar Hub:* `/services`  
   *Spoke Money Pages:* `/services/website-development`, `/services/mobile-app-development`, `/services/custom-software-development`, `/services/ecommerce-development`, `/services/ai-agents`, `/services/ai-chatbots`, `/services/business-automation`, `/services/crm-automation`, `/services/api-integration`, `/services/cloud-solutions`, `/services/ui-ux-design`, `/services/seo-optimization`, `/services/wordpress-development`.  
   *Proof & Portfolio Anchors:* `/projects` (9 live shipped websites with visuals), `/portfolio`, `/products`, `/agents`.

2. **Vertical B — Study Abroad Consultancy (Germany & Global Pathways)**  
   *Audience:* Indian undergraduate & postgraduate applicants (and parents) across Kolhapur, Pune, Western Maharashtra and India evaluating overseas degrees, public German universities and visa documentation.  
   *Pillar Hub:* `/study-abroad`  
   *Flagship Destination Guide:* `/study-abroad/germany`  
   *Spoke Service Pages:* `/study-abroad/university-selection`, `/study-abroad/admission-assistance`, `/study-abroad/visa-guidance`, `/study-abroad/student-resources`.

## 3. Funnel & Internal Linking Blueprint

- **Top-of-Funnel (TOFU — Journal Categories & Guides):** Educational articles under `/blog/study-in-germany`, `/blog/study-abroad`, `/blog/website-development`, `/blog/artificial-intelligence`, `/blog/business-automation` include structured Tables of Contents and contextual CTA links pointing into Commercial Hub pages.
- **Middle-of-Funnel (MOFU — Proof & Comparison):** `/projects`, `/portfolio`, `/products`, `/study-abroad/student-resources` answer "Who have you built for?" and "Where do I check official APS/DAAD rules?"
- **Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU — Conversion):** Service pages, Study Abroad pages and Hero CTAs deep-link cleanly into `/contact?vertical=technology` or `/contact?vertical=study-abroad` alongside direct WhatsApp (`+91 94048 95667`) and phone/email channels tracked via privacy-safe GA4 events.

## 4. Cannibalization Prevention Rules

- One primary commercial search intent per canonical URL (e.g., `/services/custom-website-development` redirects permanently via 301 to `/services/website-development`; `/services/api-development` redirects via 301 to `/services/api-integration`).
- Service commercial pages answer hiring intent ("custom website development company..."), while `/blog/*` guides answer research intent ("custom react websites vs templates...").
- Study Abroad regional info (`/study-abroad/germany`) targets destination guidance cleanly separate from service execution (`/study-abroad/visa-guidance`).
